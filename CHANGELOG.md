# Changelog

Release history for **Physics Quest: Research Run**.

Development releases use `0.Y.XX`. Increase `Y` for a major development milestone and reset `XX` to `00`. Increase `XX` for each smaller release, including fixes and interface adjustments. The changelog displays two digits for `XX`. npm metadata uses the equivalent unpadded SemVer version (for example, `0.5.3` for `0.5.03`).

The earlier v15–v18 releases are mapped below from the original README. Their legacy labels are shown alongside the new milestone numbers.

## 0.5.04

- GitHub Actions now tests and builds the PWA from source and publishes the generated output directly. Removed the duplicate checked-in `docs/` export.

## 0.5.03

- Added a dedicated changelog and linked it from the README.

## 0.5.02

- Moved the landscape touch controls up and inward, with additional clearance beyond the device’s reported safe area. Preserved touch targets of at least 48 × 48 CSS pixels.

## 0.5.01

- Moved discovery cards to timed, non-blocking notices below the upper-left HUD. Added a Discovery Journal on the main menu for revisiting the full explanations.

## 0.5.00 · Mobile controls and installable web app

- Added landscape multitouch controls, full-screen support where available, and touch-friendly buttons for discovery, lab, and results cards.
- Added home-screen installation and offline caching.

## 0.4.00 · Discovery and radiation hazards (v18)

- Enabled secret ledge collisions after discovery and queued discovery cards.
- Added radiation-themed hazards to the condensed matter and physics education levels.

## 0.3.00 · Avatars and replay variety (v17)

- Added selectable avatars, research-area power-ups, and seeded changes to eligible layouts after successful runs.

## 0.2.00 · Eight-level campaign (v16)

- Expanded the game to eight levels across three research areas.

## 0.1.00 · Research platforming (v15)

- Introduced scanner cooldowns, secret research areas, hazard stomps, and moving ledges that accelerate deeper into a level.
